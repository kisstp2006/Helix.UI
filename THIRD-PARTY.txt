Third party data in Helix.UI
============================

ThirdParty/Unicode/
  Files of the Unicode Character Database 18.0.0 (https://www.unicode.org/Public/UCD/latest/ucd/, 2026-11-16),
  unchanged, under the Unicode License v3 (ThirdParty/Unicode/LICENSE.txt). Tools/GenerateUnicode.py reads the
  property files into the tables of Source/Helix/UI/Text/Unicode/Implementation/UnicodeTables.inl (which carry the
  same notice); the tests read BidiTest.txt, BidiCharacterTest.txt and LineBreakTest.txt.
